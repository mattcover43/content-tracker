Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026.

Content Tracker is a personal, single-user tool. Its only user is its owner, Matt Cover (@mattcover43).

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads the owner's own TikTok profile basics and the public statistics of the owner's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares, post date, title).

What it does with the data

The data is written to a private spreadsheet owned by the owner. It is not sold, shared, published, or used for advertising. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.

Other people

The tool does not collect data from any other TikTok user.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in TikTok settings and delete the spreadsheet rows.
